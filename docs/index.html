FAQ

Does it read my conversations?

No. For Claude Code and Codex it counts only message counts, token numbers, timestamps and tool names from the history files on your computer, and the browser extension reports only sent, answered, length and time. Conversation content is never stored or sent anywhere.

Does talking to the cat cost money?

With a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, the cat talks through that subscription at no extra cost (it uses a little of your plan). No subscription? Start with a free Gemini key. Today’s fortune works with no AI at all. Your chats with the cat are saved on your computer and only go to the AI you connected.

Can I use it with only the browser, without Claude Code or Codex?

Yes. Choose "Browser" in the intro and add the small extension (Chrome, Edge, Whale). The cat then grows while you use claude.ai, chatgpt.com or gemini.google.com. The app walks you through the install step by step.

Is it free?

Yes. No payments, no ads.

Is this an official Anthropic, OpenAI or Google app?

No. It is an unofficial fan-made app, not affiliated with any of them.

What are hooks? Do I have to connect them?

Hooks let Claude Code / Codex tell the cat when work starts and ends. The cat grows from your history files either way, but with hooks it reacts instantly to finished replies and permission requests. Your existing settings are kept, and a backup is made.

Will it get in my way?

The cat walks along the bottom of the screen and only takes clicks on its own body. Bubbles appear only when it matters, and you can resize it, mute it, hide it for an hour, or turn on do-not-disturb.

Why does my Mac show a warning?

This beta is not signed with a paid Apple Developer account, so you allow it once in System Settings. After that it opens normally.
